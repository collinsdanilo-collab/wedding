## Venue-by-Venue Highlights

### The Artisan Hotel at Tuscan Village
*Source: `Artisan_Events_Catering_Wedding_Program_2026.pdf` (in-house catering program)*

- Deepest menu system seen so far: 3 formats (plated/buffet/stations) × 3 tiers each, plus an extensive à la carte enhancement system (raw bar, carving stations, handmade pasta station, late-night bar/breakfast items).
- Dedicated **brunch program** for welcome brunch / getting-ready / farewell brunch, with both drop-off and full-service options — worth checking whether other venues offer this at all.
- In-house "live chef enhancement" upgrades available across nearly every tier and station (+$150).
- Broad hors d'oeuvres list (30+ options) with noted gluten-free accommodations.
- **Caveat:** this document is catering pricing only — it says nothing about the physical space itself (capacity, ceremony site, rentals, restrictions), so no aesthetic/capacity/policy comparison is possible yet.

## Open Questions / Follow-Ups Needed

### The Artisan Hotel at Tuscan Village
- What is the venue/room rental fee (ceremony space + reception ballroom), and does it include tables/chairs/linens/setup?
- What is the bar/alcohol program and pricing (open bar per-person/per-hour, consumption bar, corkage for outside alcohol)?
- What is the sales tax rate applied to food & beverage?
- Is there a gratuity or service charge on plated/buffet/station catering (the doc only states 20% for full-service brunch)? If so, what percentage and is it taxed?
- What is the deposit amount, payment schedule, and cancellation policy?
- What is the overtime rate if the event runs past contracted hours?
- Is the "+$150 live chef enhancement" a flat per-station fee or per-person?
- What is the price for the Cookie Jar Station (listed with no price)?
- Maximum guest capacity for ceremony and reception spaces — can they comfortably hold 200?
- Are outside vendors (florist, DJ, photographer) required to use an in-house preferred list, or is it open?
